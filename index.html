Enter the Soul Trial

Every adventurer begins with a choice. Who are you? What path will you walk? And what will remain when the trial is over?

The Soul Trial will guide you through the creation of a complete D&D 5e character, including class, subclass, race, background, ability scores, skills, equipment, spells, and character details.

Choose Your Class

Your class determines the fundamental abilities and role of your character. Choose carefully. Your path will shape the rest of the trial.

Choose Your Subclass

Choose the specialization that defines how your class develops. Subclasses become especially important when creating characters at higher levels.

Choose Your Race

Your race grants physical and cultural traits that shape your character.
